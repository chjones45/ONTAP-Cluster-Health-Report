Results explained

Passed

The check completed and found no matching unhealthy entries, or a specialized assessment passed. This is not an overall cluster-health verdict.

Findings

The query returned a condition worth investigating, such as a LIF away from home or an offline volume. It may be intentional; a finding is not automatically an outage.

Needs review

The evidence needs interpretation: warnings, an unknown response, or incomplete diagnostic results prevent a confident health assessment. This is not a confirmed fault.

Collection error

The check or connection could not complete, for example because of a timeout, authorization failure, or CLI error. Health was not established.

Skipped

The check was not run because of release support, a failed prerequisite, or a stopped session. A skipped check is not a pass.

Information

Non-fault activity, such as confirmation that connectivity diagnostics started. An acknowledgement does not prove the diagnostic completed.

All health checks counts completed checks; errors and skips are separate. Complete describes collection, not overall health. Category badges retain their worst recorded result: error, review, findings, then passed. Some findings are inferred from table-shaped query output; inspect the retained evidence.
